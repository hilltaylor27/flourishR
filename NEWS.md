# flourishR 0.2.0


* Adds GFS metadata, country labels, wave preparation, survey weight selection and country-specific survey designs.
* Adds retention, country descriptive estimates, formula-based country regression, coefficient heterogeneity tests and random-effects meta-analysis with contextual moderators.
* Adds exact-wave lags, change scores, within-between decomposition and prospective and mixed-effects country models.
* Adds coefficient, retention and missingness plots and extends existing plots to accept country estimates.
* Adds grouped missingness summaries and a synthetic GFS workflow vignette.
* Breaking change: replaces the two original scale-scoring functions (`score_scale()` and `score_swemwbs()`) with `score_tipi()`.
